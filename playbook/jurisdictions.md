# File conventions by jurisdiction (checked 25 November 2026)

These blocks extend CLAUDE.md section 4. Kenya is already covered there. Points marked "unverified" are not confirmed by a current source. Before relying on any point in a file, check it against the official portal named. The sourcing came from web search; the official pages were not opened directly.

**Cross-cutting gaps:**
- No dedicated broker or introducer licence was confirmed for Nigeria, Türkiye, Pakistan, the UAE mainland, Ghana, Uganda or Tanzania. Introducing borrowers there needs local counsel before Chapra & Co. takes a fee.
- How each payment rail is narrated on bank statements is unverified everywhere. Learn it from the first live statement in each country, and record it in the parsing playbook.

## Nigeria
- **Registry:** CAC status report (directors and shareholders). The PSC register is public at bor.cac.gov.ng, with a threshold above 25%.
- **Tax:** the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) replaced FIRS under the 2025 tax Acts and issues the Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC). E-invoicing runs through the Merchant Buyer Solution (MBS), which gives each invoice a reference number (IRN) and a QR code. Large taxpayers (turnover of NGN 5bn and above) were due to comply by 31 July 2026.
- **Bureaus:** CRC, FirstCentral and CreditRegistry (private), and the CBN's CRMS (public).
- **Auditors:** ICAN and ANAN license accountants. Since 1 April 2026, the FRC National Audit and Assurance Firms Register is the only legal list. Verify both the firm and the signing partner.
- **Rails:** NIBSS Instant Payment (NIP), OPay, Moniepoint and PalmPay.
- **Collateral:** CBN National Collateral Registry, under the Secured Transactions in Movable Assets Act 2017.
- **Broker licensing:** the FCCPC's 2025 consumer lending rules cover intermediaries in consumer lending only. SME introduction is unverified.
- **Red flags:** forged TCCs and audit reports, and fake RC (company registration) numbers.

## Türkiye
- **Registry:** MERSİS and the Trade Registry Gazette (Ticaret Sicil Gazetesi), plus the faaliyet belgesi (activity certificate) from the chamber. Check e-Devlet barcoded documents at turkiye.gov.tr/belge-dogrulama. UBO declarations are filed with GİB under Communiqué 529 and are not public.
- **Tax:** the e-Vergi Levhası (tax plate), with an online check. e-Fatura and e-Arşiv invoices.
- **Bureaus:** KKB (Findeks) and the TBB Risk Center.
- **Auditors:** KGK, with the "Bağımsız Denetçi Sorgulama" lookup on e-Devlet.
- **Rails:** FAST. The per-transaction limit is TRY 300,000 from 26 August 2026.
- **Collateral:** TARES pledge registry, under Law 6750.
- **Broker licensing:** no intermediary licence was found. Carrying on banking activities without a licence under Law 5411 is a criminal offence.
- **Red flags:** fake or "twin" cheques. Check the QR code through the KKB or Findeks cheque report. Altered gazette PDFs (unverified).

## Brazil
- **Registry:** the certidão simplificada (company certificate) from the state Junta Comercial, and the QSA (partners and administrators) on the Receita Federal's CNPJ record.
- **Tax:** the federal debt certificate (CND or CRF) issued jointly by the Receita Federal and PGFN, with its control code checked on gov.br. NF-e invoices can be checked at SEFAZ by their access key.
- **Bureaus:** Serasa Experian, Boa Vista, SPC Brasil and Quod (private), and the central bank's SCR (public).
- **Auditors:** CRC/CFC, plus the CVM register for auditors of listed companies.
- **Rails:** Pix, typically narrated "PIX RECEBIDO" and "PIX ENVIADO" (unverified).
- **Collateral:**
  - receivables: registrars CERC, B3, Núclea, TAG and CRDC;
  - fiduciary liens: RTD registry, under Lei 14.711/2023;
  - agricultural: CPR registration.
- **Broker licensing:** the introducer acts as a "correspondente no país" under CMN Res. 4.935. Existing contracts must comply with Res. Conjunta 17/2025 by 28 November 2026.
- **Red flags:** "duplicata fria" (invoices with no real sale behind them) and double assignment of the same receivable. Edited CND PDFs: always re-check the control code.

## Pakistan
- **Registry:** SECP Form A (annual return, with shareholders and directors), Form 29 (changes of directors and officers) and Form 45 (beneficial owners). Ask for SECP certified copies.
- **Tax:** FBR NTN and the Active Taxpayer List (ATL). Check ATL status by sending "ATL <7-digit NTN>" by SMS to 9966. Sales tax e-invoicing operates under SRO 709/2025.
- **Bureaus:** Tasdeeq and DataCheck (private), and the SBP's eCIB (public).
- **Auditors:** the ICAP list of firms with a satisfactory QCR rating, and the AOB for public interest audits.
- **Rails:** Raast and IBFT, plus JazzCash and Easypaisa.
- **Collateral:** the SECP Secured Transactions Registry, and the register of charges for companies.
- **Broker licensing:** unverified.
- **Red flags:** edited ATL or NTN screenshots, so re-check by SMS. Post-dated cheques offered as comfort.

## United Arab Emirates
- **Registry:**
  - Mainland: the economic department trade licence (the Dubai Unified Licence carries a QR code) and the National Economic Register.
  - DIFC and ADGM: public registers, UBO regulations, and a certificate of incumbency.
  - A free zone company missing from the mainland register is normal.
- **Tax:** a TRN check on the FTA portal, plus VAT and Corporate Tax registration certificates. E-invoicing is mandatory in phases from 1 January 2027.
- **Bureau:** Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB).
- **Auditors:** the Ministry of Economy auditor register. DIFC and ADGM run their own regimes.
- **Rails:** Aani instant payments and UAEFTS.
- **Collateral:** EIRC, the former Emirates Movable Collateral Registry, under Federal Law 4/2020.
- **Broker licensing:**
  - DIFC: "Arranging Credit and Advising on Credit" needs DFSA authorisation.
  - ADGM: "Arranging Credit" needs FSRA authorisation.
  - Mainland: unverified.
- **Red flags:** fake trade licences (mismatched numbers, outdated logos) and forged audited accounts.

## Saudi Arabia
- **Registry:** the Ministry of Commerce Commercial Register, under the CR Law in force since April 2025. UBO data is confidential.
- **Tax:** the ZATCA Zakat Certificate, checkable online by CR or TIN. Fatoora e-invoicing (Phase 2 wave 25 integrates by 1 February 2027).
- **Bureaus:** SIMAH and Bayan.
- **Auditors:** SOCPA (public register unverified).
- **Rails:** sarie and mada.
- **Collateral:** the Unified Registry for Commercial Pledges, under the Commercial Pledge Law 2018.
- **Broker licensing:** SAMA licenses digital brokerage under the Finance Support Activities rules, with minimum capital of SAR 2m.
- **Red flags:** forged property deeds and fake contracts, in some cases with a bank employee colluding (a SAR 493m case).

## Ghana
- **Registry:** ORC, under Act 992. Beneficial ownership is filed on Forms BO1 to BO4 and is not publicly searchable.
- **Tax:** the GRA electronic Tax Clearance Certificate. Under E-VAT, only invoices cleared by GRA with a QR code are valid.
- **Bureaus:** XDS Data, Dun & Bradstreet and My Credit Score. HudsonPrice's licence was revoked.
- **Auditors:** ICAG "List of Licensed Firms in Good Standing" (31 December 2025).
- **Rails:** MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money and GhIPSS GIP.
- **Collateral:** the Bank of Ghana Collateral Registry, under Act 1052.
- **Broker licensing:** none found. Digital credit providers need a BoG licence.
- **Red flags:** accounts signed by firms not on the ICAG list.

## Uganda
- **Registry:** URSB company and beneficial ownership registers.
- **Tax:** the URA Tax Clearance Certificate, and EFRIS e-invoicing and e-receipts.
- **Bureaus:** four licensed by BoU, including Metropol and Creditinfo. Check the current BoU list.
- **Auditors:** the ICPAU online register (275 firms in 2026).
- **Rails:** MTN MoMo and Airtel Money. On Kenyan statements, Ugandan numbers start +256.
- **Collateral:** the SIMPO registry, with public search at simpo.ursb.go.ug.
- **Broker licensing:** UMRA licenses money lenders and digital credit providers. Brokers are unverified.
- **Red flags:** forged share transfers and resolutions filed at URSB, and forged bank stamps.

## Tanzania
- **Registry:** BRELA ORS (ors.brela.go.tz). Beneficial ownership is mandatory under s.459A, and other filings are blocked until it is filed.
- **Tax:** the TRA electronic Tax Clearance Certificate, issued through IDRAS with a verification QR code. EFD and VFD fiscal receipts.
- **Bureaus:** Creditinfo Tanzania and Dun & Bradstreet, plus the Bank of Tanzania's databank.
- **Auditors:** the NBAA list of active firms.
- **Rails:** TIPS, M-Pesa, Mixx by Yas and Airtel Money. On Kenyan statements, Tanzanian numbers start +255.
- **Collateral:** BRELA registers company charges. A collateral registry under the Secured Transactions (Movable Property) Act 2026 is planned; its commencement is unverified.
- **Broker licensing:** lending needs a BoT Tier 2 licence under the Microfinance Act 2018. Brokers are unverified.
- **Red flags:** a TCC without a working QR code. For any "Tanzanian sourcing" claim, ask for TRA-registered supplier invoices and TIPS or SWIFT evidence of payment.
